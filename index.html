About Me

Engineering through design thinking

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student minoring in Business Administration, excited to create, explore, impact, and simply learn how things work and build cool things. I have experience through the whole design process — from identifying the problem, to market research, through CAD modeling, fabrication, and final assembly. I approach each challenge with curiosity and persistence through countless iterations. I've led teams through that process too, not just built things on my own. In the long run, I want to combine engineering and business to build and innovate, creating solutions that make everyday life more efficient, accessible, and sustainable, and that make a real difference in people's everyday lives. I'm still exploring the sea of opportunities and fields engineering has to offer, but I'm always ready to dive in through hands-on problem solving and expand my understanding and possibilities. I'm hoping that projects, co-ops, and various other opportunities will help me find my place in engineering.
